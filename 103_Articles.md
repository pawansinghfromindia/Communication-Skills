> A **countable noun** is something we can count individually.

Example : book, pen, student, car, apple, chair, computer etc

Countable nouns can usually use: `a/an`, `one`, `two`, `many`, `few`, `several`

```
We can say: one book, two books, three books
Therefore, book is countable.

I have a book.       Singular
I have three books.  Plural
```

> An **uncountable** noun refers to something that we don't normally count as individual units.

Examples : water, milk, rice, sugar, information, advice, knowledge, furniture, money etc

How do we count them?
```
We use a unit:
- a glass of water
- two bottles of water
- a piece of advice
- two pieces of advice
- a piece of information
- three pieces of information
```

  <summary> 2. <b> Definite Article : <code> the </code> </b> </summary>

<br/>

> The definite article (`the`) is used before a noun to indicate that personal, certain, or former acquaintance;
 which has already been discussed.

`The` is used with countable Nouns (Singular noun & Plural noun) and uncountable nouns.


**Uses of Definite Article `the`**

- Use `the` for things that have been mentioned already. 
- Use `the` when there is something unique, even if not mentioned already.
- Use `the` when we are expressing a statement for a particular person or object.

#### 1. Before a noun already known to subject : `the`

When we talk about something that has already been referred to in the prior sentence, we use "The".

Examples:
```
A cat got in honor of our dog today. The dog wins the battle.
Mr. David is wearing a suit at the party.   - "the" refer to the party
```

#### 2. Before unique nouns : `the` 

Before a unique or universal things, we do use `the`.
All these Universal things or Places are certain and would not change. 

Examples:
```
The Sun,
The Moon,
The Arabian Ocean,
The Bermuda Triangle, 
The Eiffel Tower,
The Himalayas, etc. 
```

#### 3. Before superlative adjectives : `the`


#### 4. Before proper nouns : `the`

We use `the` before proper nouns such as Nation's Name, Plural names of nations, some titles, some associations, 
some structures, papers, masterpieces, explicit family names, mountains, waterways, oceans, gatherings of islands, 
deserts, etc

Examples:
```
The Indian sea,
The Bay of Bengal,
 The Netherlands,
The Caspian sea,
The United States of America,
The Taj Mahal,
The Times of India,
The Suez Canal,
The Guptas,
etc
```

#### 5. With Nationalities : `the`

We use `the` before any nationality to emphasize that word regardless of whether nationality is in the plural.

Examples:
```
The Mughals and The British controlled India for a long time.
The Indians.
The Chinese.   
The Americans.
The Russians.
```

#### 6. Mentions of services and systems : `the`

Examples:
```
Switch on the television to watch the newest show.
We must go to the police station to report the crime.
You should take this to the buyer's court.
```

#### 7. Comparative proclamations : `the`

When we compare two things with each other motivational advice or something else, must be used `the` in
such a sentence. 

```
The more he works, the more he will be successful.
The more we travel, the more we shall fall in love with Nature.
```

<details>
  <summary>  Rules for using Articles <code> a/an/the </code> </summary>

<br/>

#### Rule 1

If  Noun comes before an Adjective and an Adjective comes before Adverb then we use article before the Adverb.

Examples:
```
Radha is a girl.
Radha is a very good girl.
Radha is an ideal girl.
Radha is an intelligent girl.
This is the horse.
This is the strongest horse.
```

#### Rule 2

In many situations Articles come after such as All, Both, Such, Double, Half, Rather, etc.

Examples:
```
All the boys.
Such a woman.
half an hour.
Double the amount.
```
