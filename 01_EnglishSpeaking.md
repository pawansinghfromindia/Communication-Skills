# English Speaking - Roadmap

| Level       | Focus                     | Outcome                                      |
| ----------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1** | Basic sentence formation  | Speak simple sentences correctly             |
| **Level 2** | Fluency & conversation    | Speak without translating every sentence     |
| **Level 3** | Professional English      | Meetings, discussions, explaining ideas      |
| **Level 4** | Presentation & leadership | Teach, present, handle questions confidently |


 ## Level 1 - Build the Foundation
 
<details>
  <summary> <b> Basics </b> </summary>

- Sentence structure
- Basic grammar
- Tenses
- is/am/are
- has/have
- do/does/did
- Prepositions
- Question formation
- Common mistakes made by Indian English speakers
- Everyday vocabulary

Don't memorize grammar rules endlessly.

</details>
<!----------------------------->


## Level 2 - Start Thinking in English

<details>
  <summary> <b> Daily situations </b> </summary>

## Level 3 - Professional English

<details>
  <summary> <b> Meetings, Calls </b> </summary>

<br/>

You'll learn how to say: 
```
- I'd like to add something here.
- Could you please clarify that point?
- From my perspective...
- I agree with your point, however...
- Let me explain how this works.
- There seems to be an issue with...
- I'll take care of this.
- Can we discuss this separately?
```

</details>
<!----------------------------->

## Level 4 - Presentation & Teaching

<details>
  <summary> <b> Training & Learning </b> </summary>

Training to speak to an audience. <br/>
Learning a simple presentation structure:

1. **Opening**
```"Good morning everyone. Today I'd like to talk about..."```

2. **Agenda**
```"I'll cover three main points."```

3. **Explanation**
```"Let's start with..."```

4. **Example**
```"Let me explain this with an example."```

5. **Transition**
```"Now let's move on to the next point."```

6. **Conclusion**
```"To summarize..."```

7. **Questions**
```"I'd be happy to answer any questions."```

This gives us a structure to fall back on, which dramatically improves confidence.


</details>

## Plans and Execution

<details>
  <summary> <b> 45 minutes time of your day </b> </summary>

<br/>

> **Daily English Routine** : We don't need 3 hours every day.

| 45–60 minutes |                                                                             |
|--------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 15 min | **Learn** Grammar + vocabulary + useful expressions                                |
| 15 min | **Speak** Speak aloud about a topic e.g. "What did I do today?"                    |
| 10 min | **Listen** Listen to natural English from interviews, presentations, podcasts, etc |
| 10 min | **Think in English** Look around and mentally describe things                      |
| 5 min  | **Review** Write down 3-5 mistakes or new expressions.                             |


</details>

<details>
  <summary> <b> Rules </b> </summary>

<br/>

> **Don't wait until your English is perfect before speaking.**

Approach
```
Broken English → Correct English → Natural English → Confident English
Not:
Learn everything → Become perfect → Start speaking
```
