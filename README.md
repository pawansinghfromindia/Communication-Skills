<details>
  <summary> <b> 1.  Sentence structure </b> </summary>

<br/>

> **Subject + Verb + Object**
```
I + learn + English.
She + reads + books.
They + play + cricket.
We + discuss + the problem.
```

> **Subject**

_Who is doing the action?_ <br/>
e.g. : `I, You, He, She, We, They, Any name etc`

> **Verb** 

_What is the action? or doing anything_ <br/>
e.g. : `learn, read, play, discuss, work, eat, speak etc`

> **Object**

_What receives the action? or about Whom?_ <br/>
e.g. : `English, books, cricket, the problem, etc`

Example : Now let's make professional sentences
```
Step 1 - I have a meeting.
Step 2 - I have a meeting at 3 PM.
Step 3 - I have a meeting at 3 PM with my team.
Step 4 - I have a meeting at 3 PM with my team to discuss the project.
Step 4 - I have a meeting at 3 PM with my team to discuss progress of the project.

This is how, we simple → longer → natural → professional
```
