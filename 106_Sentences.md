# Sentences 

<details>
  <summary> <b> Introduction - Sentences </b> </summary>

<br/>


> **A sentence is a group of words that expresses a complete thought.**

**Sentences always have a _subject_ and a _verb_.**

| Subject      | Verb         |  Sentence                 |
|--------------|--------------|---------------------------|
| I            | am working   | I am working.             |
| They         | are studying | They are studying.        |
| He/She       | is reading   | He/She is reading.        |
| The Children | are playing  | The children are playing. |


</details>
<!----------------------------->

## Four Kinds of Sentence

<details>
  <summary> <b> 1. Declarative sentence </b> </summary>

<br/>

> A **declarative sentence** makes a **statement**. 

```
It is raining.
Rohit likes cricket.
The school bell was ringing.
The children are playing with the dog.
Shiva is in Banarasi.
```

Note: A declarative sentence ends with a **period** means fullstop.

</details>
<!----------------------------->

<details>
  <summary> <b> 2. Interrogative sentence </b> </summary>

<br/>

> An **interrogative sentence** asks a **question**. 

```
What is your name?
Where were you?
Why is the sky blue?
Who is talking to the teacher?
Are you happy?
```

Note: An interrogative sentence ends with a **question mark (?)** instead of a period.


</details>
<!----------------------------->

<details>
  <summary> <b> Exclamatory sentence </b> </summary>

<br/>

> An **exclamatory sentence** makes a very strong statement called an **exclamation**. 

It shows a strong feeling/emotions such as surprise, excitement, sorrow or anger. 

```
How beautiful she is!
The silly boy!
What a heavy day!
```

Note: An exclamatory sentence ends with an **exclamation mark (!)** instead of a period.

</details>
<!----------------------------->

<details>
  <summary> <b> Imperative sentence </b> </summary>

<br/>

> An **imperative sentence** gives an **order**.

```
Ask Rohan to come and see me.
Don't tell me lies.
Please leave.
Go to your room!
Speak up!
```

Note: An imperative sentence can end with an exclamation point (!) if the order is very firm otherwise 
generally ends with a period(fullstop). 

</details>
<!----------------------------->

### Sentences with Objects

<details>
  <summary> <b> Sentence with no object </b> </summary>

<br/>

> An intransitive doesn't need a direct object to complete their meaning.

| subject      | intransitive verb | sentence |
|--------------|---------|----------------------|
| The baby     | slept   | The baby slept.      |
| The children | laughed | The children laughed.|
| He           | arrived | He arrived.          |
| The sun      | rose.   | The sun rose.        |
| The door     | opened. | The door opened.     |

</details>
<!----------------------------->


<details>
  <summary> <b> Sentences with Objects </b> </summary>

<br/>


> The **subject** of a sentence often does something to another person or thing.

> The person or thing that receives the action of the subject is called the **object** of the verb. 

> Verbs that have objects are called **transitive verbs**. 

| subject	  | transitive verb	|  object                 | sentence                                |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| Dad	      | is reading	    | a book                  |  Dad is reading a book.                 |
| I	        | am cooking	    | dinner                  | I am cooking dinner.                    |
| You       | have broken	    | my new toy              | You have broken my new toy.             |
| Mom	      | likes	          | her new car             | Mom likes her new car.                  |
| She       | has forgotten   |	her bag                 | She has forgotton her bag.              |
| The dog	  | licked          | her face                | The dog licked her face.                |
| Our ball	| hit             | a window                | Our ball hit a window.                  |
| They      | visited	        | the museum              | They visited the museum.                |
| Radha	    | is sewing	      | a dress for her doll    | Radha is sewing a dress for her doll.   |
| Uncle Sam |	 sent	          | a package to his friend | Uncle Sam sent a package to his friend. |


</details>
<!----------------------------->



<details>
  <summary> <b> Verbs with no object </b> </summary>

<br/>

Some verbs don't have an object. 

A verb that does not have an object is called an **intransitive verb**. 

Here are some sentences with intransitive verbs. 

```
It is snowing.
We have already eaten.
Dad always drives carefully.
Mr. X usually walks to work.
The sun is shining.	
I don't know.	
The man smiled.	
He always dresses very smartly.
```

</details>
<!----------------------------->
