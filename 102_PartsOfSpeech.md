## Parts of Speech

> **Words are divided into different kinds or classes, are known as parts of speech.**

English grammar consists of 8 parts of speech : `nouns`, `pronouns`, `verbs`, `adjectives`, `adverbs`, `prepositions`, `conjunctions`, and `interjections`. 

Each part of speech has its own unique function and plays a vital role in constructing meaningful sentences. 

<details>
  <summary> 1. <b> Nouns </b> </summary>

<br/>

> **Nouns are words that name a person, place, animal, thing, event or idea.**

Example : Ram, Mumbai, Lion, Book, love etc

Example : Person - Rahul, teacher, doctor, student etc <br/>
Example : Place - India, school, office, Delhi etc <br/>
Example : Thing - phone, computer, book, table etc <br/>
Example : Animal - Cow, Tiger, Lion, Elephant etc <br/>
Example : Idea - happiness, freedom, knowledge, honesty etc <br/>


```
Rahul works in an office.  Noun = Rahul(Sub), Office(Obj)
```

</details>
<!----------------------------------------->

<details>
  <summary> 2. <b> Pronouns </b> </summary>

<br/>

> **Pronouns are words that replace nouns to avoid repetition.**

Examples : he, she, it, they, we etc <br/>
Examples : me, him, her, us, them etc

```
Rahul is a software engineer. Rahul works in Bangalore. Rahul likes Python. 
Rahul is a software engineer. He works in Bangalore. He likes Python.

Here Noun = Rahul, Pronoun = He
```

</details>
<!----------------------------------------->

<details>
  <summary> 3. <b> Verbs </b> </summary>

<br/>

> **Verbs are words that describe actions, states, or occurrences.**

What is the subject doing? <br/>
Examples: eat, play, sing, talk, study, learn, run, sleep, think, happen etc

What is the subject's state? <br/>
Examples : is/am/are, was/were, has/have/had, can/could, will/shall, should/would etc 

```
I work from home.      verb = work
She speaks English.    verb = speak
They played cricket.   verb = play

He/She is happy.       verb = is
I am tired.            verb = am
They are sad.          verb = are
```

</details>
<!----------------------------------------->

<details>
  <summary> 4. <b> Adjectives </b> </summary>

<br/>

> **Adjectives are words that describe or modify nouns.**

Like What kind?, Which one?, How many?

Examples : intelligent, good, bad, beautiful, smart, big, small, difficult, easy, interesting, 
expensive, new, old, red

```
I bought a new laptop.    adj = new   -> what kind? 
She is a smart student.   adj = smart -> what kind?
He has a red car.         adj = red   -> which one? 
I have three books.       adj = three -> how many? 
```

</details>
<!----------------------------------------->

<details>
  <summary> 5. <b> Adverbs </b> </summary>

<br/>

> **Adverbs are words that modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs.**

Like How? When? Where? How often?

Examples : quickly, slowly, very, quite, quickly, carefully, always, never, often, sometimes, really, yesterday, 
today, tomorrow

```
He runs quickly.            adverb = quickly  -> How?
I will call you tomorrow.   adverb = tomorrow -> when?
I always study Maths.       adverb = always   -> How often?
They reached there late.    adverb = late     -> when?

```


</details>
<!----------------------------------------->

<details>
  <summary> 6. <b> Prepositions </b> </summary>

<br/>

> **Prepositions are words that show the relationship between a noun or pronoun and another word in the sentence.**

Examples: in, on, at, under, above, below, over, beside, between, from, to, with, for, about

```
The book is on the table.        preposition = on
I'm in the room.                 preposition = in
She is at home.                  preposition = at
He went to school.               preposition = to
I am talking with my friend.     preposition = with
The cat is under the table.      preposition = under
He was standing beside me.       preposition = beside
Choose between two people.       preposition = between
I'm from India.                  preposition = from 
```

</details>
<!----------------------------------------->

<details>
  <summary> 7. <b> Conjunctions </b> </summary>

<br/>

> Conjunctions are words that connect words, phrases, or clauses.

Examples: and, or, but, because, so, although, if, while

```
I study Maths and Science.             conjunction = and
I wanted to go, but I was busy.        conjunction = but
I stayed home because it was raining.  conjunction = because
Study hard or you may fail.            conjunction = or
```

</details>
<!----------------------------------------->


<details>
  <summary> 8. <b> Interjections </b> </summary>

<br/>

> Interjections are words or phrases that express strong emotions or reactions.

Examples: Wow!, Ouch!, Hey!, Oh!, Oh no!, Alias! Wow! Ouch! Ah! Hurray!

```
Wow! That's amazing.      Interjection = Wow!
Ouch! That hurts.         Interjection =
Oh! I forgot my phone.    Interjection =
Hurray! We won the match. Interjection =
Interjection =
Interjection = 
```

</details>

**Wow! The smart boy quickly finished his homework in the classroom because he had an exam tomorrow.**
```
Wow! = Interjection -> emotion
the  = Article(Determiner) -> specifies boy
smart = Adjective  -> describes boy
boy  = Noun   -> person
quickly = Adverb -> descibes finish
finished = Verb  -> action
his = pronoun/determiner -> refers to the boy
homework = Noun -> thing
in = Preposition -> relationship /position
the = Article(determiner) -> specifies classroom
classroom = Noun -> place
because = Conjunction -> connects ideas
he = Pronoun -> replaces boy
had = verb -> state
an = Article(determiner) -> specifies exam
exam = Noun -> thing/event
tomorrow = Adverb -> tells when
```

Understanding the different parts of speech and their functions is essential for constructing grammatically correct sentences and conveying your thoughts effectively.

Each part of speech contributes to the overall meaning and structure of a sentence, allowing you to express yourself clearly and precisely.

</details>
